## 中文 Results 草稿

在该模拟数据集中，第 28 天对照组、处理 A 组和处理 B 组的平均响应分别为 48.2、59.4 和 61.1 a.u.（每组 n = 8）。与对照组相比，处理 A 组的均值差为 11.2 a.u.（95% CI 6.7 至 15.6），处理 B 组的均值差为 12.9 a.u.（95% CI 7.2 至 18.6）。均值差区间采用 Welch-Satterthwaite 不等方差方法计算。这些描述仅概括模拟观测及其不确定性；未进行显著性检验，不能据此提出真实机制或因果结论。

## English Results draft

In this simulated dataset, the mean day-28 responses were 48.2, 59.4, and 61.1 a.u. for the control, treatment A, and treatment B groups, respectively (n = 8 per group). Relative to control, the mean difference was 11.2 a.u. for treatment A (95% CI 6.7 to 15.6) and 12.9 a.u. for treatment B (95% CI 7.2 to 18.6). Mean-difference intervals used the Welch-Satterthwaite unequal-variance method. These statements summarize only the simulated observations and their uncertainty. No significance test was performed, and the data do not support a real mechanistic or causal claim.
