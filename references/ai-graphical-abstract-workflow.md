# AI-assisted graphical abstracts: scope and evidence

Read before planning, revising or auditing an AI-assisted graphical abstract or mechanism schematic. The approved RStudio/Origin routes remain active; reasoning, wording or layout assistance does not authorize an unrelated image-generation backend.

## Policy before a submission candidate

Identify the journal, article type and intended use. Check its current official rules for graphical abstracts, generated images, image integrity, confidentiality, permissions and disclosure when external access is authorized. Practitioner articles and published examples do not constitute permission.

Record the policy source and check date. If eligibility is unknown, mark the work as an internal concept draft with submission status unverified. Separate internal design usefulness from permission to submit an asset.

Do not send underlying datasets, unpublished images, private manuscripts or review material to an external generation service through this workflow. No API account, upload, subscription or image-provider integration is introduced by installing this reference.

## Define the message

Record the intended reader, the single takeaway, required entities and relationships, reading direction and evidence boundary. Identify what is measured, inferred or hypothetical and what the diagram must not imply.

Choose only details needed for that message. Omitting detail for clarity must not remove a qualification that changes the result. Learn general organization from public examples without reproducing protected artwork or distinctive compositions.

## Bounded assistance

AI can help compare wording, identify unexplained terminology, propose arrangements and check the brief for contradictions. Scientific claims, component names and links must remain grounded in supplied evidence. Do not invent measurements, pathways, molecular structures, clinical effects or citations to make the image convincing.

Use neutral context and semantic emphasis from the existing palette system. Specify labels before construction; reserve space for evidence-status markers and necessary caveats. Multiple alternatives are useful only when they resolve an actual communication choice.

For production, translate the reviewed brief into saved R geometry using the [vector workflow](vector-schematic-workflow.md) and [style rules](vector-schematic-style-rules.md), then execute and inspect it in RStudio, or construct equivalent editable Origin objects when suitable under the authorized Origin workflow. If that route cannot express a required feature, state the limitation and seek a task-specific exception; do not silently generate a substitute bitmap.

## Review and provenance

Check every entity, relationship, direction, scale implication, label, abbreviation and causal statement against the available source. Verify rights for any supplied external icons or assets and whether the destination requires disclosure of AI assistance. Use [rendered QA](rendered-qa.md) for the same production checks as other figures.

Extend the existing provenance record with the assistance actually used: tool/model identity when known, date, relevant prompt or brief, rights information, accepted suggestions and human corrections. Do not record a generation event or provider that was not used. Do not retain confidential prompts in a distributable skill.

The author remains responsible for scientific review. A verified layout, editable SVG or readable preview does not establish the mechanism or journal eligibility. Report those states separately.
