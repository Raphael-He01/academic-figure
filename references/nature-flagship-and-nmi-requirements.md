# Nature and NMI: differences by submission stage

Read only for the flagship Nature journal or Nature Machine Intelligence (NMI). Use [the existing journal profile](journal-requirements.md) for widths, ordinary text, panel lettering and stroke guidance already recorded there; this page does not duplicate that preset.

Status: as of 2026-10-26, this is an offline synthesis of the supplied packages, not a new official-page verification. Re-verify against the official page before submission. The Nature source reports an earlier 2026-08-08 check; historical NMI legend guidance must not be presented as a current rule.

## Determine the stage first

For initial review, readable figures can be embedded with the manuscript; missing separate production artwork is not by itself a failure. Keep figures and their legends easy to inspect together. Revisions follow the editor's request as well as public instructions. After acceptance in principle, distinguish Main production files from Extended Data (ED).

Data interpretation, source traceability and image integrity apply at every stage. An initial-review allowance does not permit unreadable labels or unsupported statistics.

## Flagship Nature: additional checks

As of 2026-10-26; re-verify against the official page before submission:

- The supplied source gives a complete-legend limit below 250 words. Count the whole title-plus-panel description, not each panel independently. Consult [caption rules](caption-and-results.md) for self-contained statistical definitions.
- Main production art should retain editable components and embedded resources. The supplied guide lists editable PDF/AI/EPS, with additional prepared formats including plain SVG. A PNG/TIFF/JPEG preview is not automatically an acceptable final Main file.
- The supplied Main-file guidance recommends a file smaller than 50 MB. Confirm current upload constraints and accepted formats rather than treating this as proof of eligibility.
- ED follows another route: RGB, at most 300 dpi, at most 10 MB per file, and a single page per item with room for legend/footnotes; the source lists JPEG preferentially and TIFF/EPS alternatives. Confirm naming requirements at upload time.
- Keep scale bars and labels editable where possible. Record image crop, tonal changes, pseudocolour, calibration, joins and raw-file linkage in the QA record.

The source's assignment of a 170 mm height to production art conflicts with the stage-specific records already in the existing journal page. Do not overwrite that record or combine conflicting heights into a new universal limit. Resolve the applicable height from the current editor/stage instructions.

Avoid importing an unconditional ban on all grids or all coloured annotations from a summary. Assess the exact destination's rules and scientific reading needs; preserve accessible and explained encodings.

## Nature Machine Intelligence

As of 2026-10-26; re-verify against the official page before submission:

- Article and Analysis formats in the supplied contract allow up to 6 Main display items, counting figures and tables together.
- The corresponding ED budget is 10 figures and tables combined. A multi-panel figure is not a justification for hiding unreadable content inside a single item.
- The production line specifies at least 300 dpi and a maximum width of 180 mm. Apply these to the appropriate deliverable and preserve editable vector graphs; do not reinterpret them as a generic all-format rasterization command.
- A 2018 official brief guide is reported to advise legends below 300 English words. The supplied source says its more recent live pages did not give a standalone per-legend cap. Treat that number as historical advice, not a freshly verified mandatory limit; do not substitute Nature's cap.

For a different article type, stop borrowing the Article/Analysis budget and consult its own instructions. This merge did not inspect unrelated NMI article-format requirements.

## Submission record

For each figure, retain stage, requested format, applicable rule/source date, actual file evidence, unresolved differences and required action in the existing manifest/QA record. Keep a preview, an editable master and a submission file distinguishable even when they share a stem.

Official verification entry points and the package file inventory remain centralized in [sources](sources.md). Consult the applicable journal's initial-review, final-production and image-integrity instructions; a stored link is not evidence of a current successful check.
